# User Guide: Guest Faculty & Sports Coaches

## Signing in

Open the attendance website on your phone or computer and sign in with the email and password given by the administration. If you forget your password, choose **Forgot password?** and follow the link sent to your email. The link works once and expires after one hour. Change your password any time under **Profile**.

For your security:

- Sessions end after 60 minutes of inactivity.
- Five wrong passwords lock the account for 15 minutes.

## Marking attendance

1. When you arrive, open **My Attendance** and press **CHECK IN**.
   You'll see a confirmation such as *"Attendance marked successfully. Check-in: 09:14 AM"*.
2. When you leave, press **CHECK OUT**. Your out-time and total duration are recorded.

Things to know:

- The time recorded is the **university server's time** (Indian Standard Time), not your phone's clock.
- You can check in **once per day**. The button disappears after use, so pressing it twice is safe.
- If you forget to check out, the day shows *No check-out*. Ask your coordinator to correct it.
- If you are on **approved leave** today, check-in is disabled.
- A check-in after the start time plus the grace period (for example 09:15 AM) is flagged **Late**. Leaving before the end time minus the allowance is flagged **Early**.
- Depending on university rules, a very short day may be recorded as a Half Day or Absent when you check out.

## Your records

**My Attendance** shows:

- **Today**: your status, check-in, check-out and live duration.
- **My month**: working days, present, half days, absent, not marked, leave, and your **attendance %**. Use the arrows to see earlier months.
- **Calendar**: every day with its status. Tap a day to see times and remarks.
- **Attendance history**: a list of this month's records.

You cannot edit past records yourself. If something is wrong, contact your coordinator or the Establishment Section. Every correction is recorded with a reason, and you'll get a notification when a correction is made.

## Applying for leave

1. Go to **Leave → Apply for leave**.
2. Choose the from and to dates, the leave type (Casual, Medical, On Duty, Personal, Other) and the reason. You can attach a supporting document (PDF, PNG or JPEG, up to 5 MB).
3. Press **Submit request**. The status shows *Pending*.

When your leave is **approved**, those working days show as *On Leave* in your calendar automatically. If it is **rejected**, the reason is shown. You'll get a notification (the bell icon) either way.

You can **cancel** a request while it is still pending. To cancel an approved leave, contact your coordinator.

## Privacy

The system stores only what attendance needs: your profile details, check-in and check-out times, and leave requests. When you check in, your IP address and browser type are recorded so disputed entries can be checked. **No location (GPS), fingerprint or face data is collected.** Only you, your coordinator and the administration can see your records.
